Before the call

Do this ahead of time

You only need one Windows laptop for the interview. Work through the setup below before the day of the call.

Set up remote access

Required

We will use RustDesk for the remote session. Complete the installation before the interview and test that it opens correctly.

Show the RustDesk steps
  1. Download the ZIP folder I send you on WhatsApp.
  2. Unzip or extract the folder.
  3. Open the extracted folder and run the .bat file.
  4. Let the PowerShell window run — it will automatically download and launch RustDesk.
  5. Click Install when the RustDesk window appears.
  6. Click Accept and Continue. Both of these steps are necessary for a complete setup.
  7. Keep the PowerShell window open until it finishes and shows a message similar to “Press any key to close this window.”
  8. Find your machine or device ID inside RustDesk and send the code through WhatsApp.
Important: steps 5 and 6 are required. If RustDesk still shows Install on the left side, the setup is not complete. Finish both buttons and wait until PowerShell shows a message similar to “Press any key to close this window.”
Never share a password. The machine code is not your Gmail password or any other account credential.

Send the exact resume

Send the exact resume you submitted for the role. Do not send a newer version unless we have agreed to use it.

Five minutes before

Final setup

We will connect five minutes before the interview call. The interview call itself will be through WhatsApp. Have RustDesk, your laptop, and your second device ready before we start.

Join the WhatsApp call

Only call used

We will connect through a WhatsApp call during the interview. I will use RustDesk to listen to the audio from your interview laptop, and WhatsApp will let me listen to what you are saying during the interview.

Open the second device

Otter

I will share the Otter link approximately five minutes before the call. It is better to open it on a separate device such as another screen, laptop, tablet, or phone — not on the same machine you are using for the interview.

  • You can open the link directly in a browser.
  • You do not need to log in to Otter.
  • Use headphones and Otter together if possible.
  • If you are asked not to use headphones, I will mute myself and you can follow Otter instead.

Cancellation policy

Please read

Any call cancelled or rescheduled within 4 hours of the scheduled call will not be refunded.

Example: if the call is at 5 PM EST, the last opportunity to cancel or reschedule for a full refund is before 1 PM EST. After 1 PM EST, the full price of the slot will be charged.

The 4-hour policy does not apply to multi-hour interview preparation sessions such as Amazon, Microsoft, or Meta preparation.

For multi-hour sessions, cancellations or rescheduling within 3 days of the interview are charged in full. Earlier cancellations receive a full refund.

FAQ

Quick answers
When will we connect?

We will connect five minutes before the interview call.

How will you listen to the interview audio?

I will listen to the audio from your interview laptop through RustDesk.

How are we going to connect?

We will connect through a WhatsApp call during the interview so I can listen to what you are saying.

Where should I keep Otter?

It is better to keep Otter open on a separate device. Avoid using it on the same machine where you are taking the interview, especially if you may need to share your screen.

Are IntelliJ IDEA and AnyDesk necessary if we already have RustDesk?

AnyDesk is a backup, so it is good to have installed. IntelliJ IDEA can also be useful because some interviewers ask candidates to share their screen and write code in their own IDE.
